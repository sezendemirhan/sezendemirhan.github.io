About

Researching bodies, relationships and inequalities

My research brings empirical inquiry into conversation with questions of gender, health and ethics. I am particularly interested in how social relationships, moral expectations and institutional contexts shape experiences of living organ donation and transplantation.

My work also engages with animal ethics, xenotransplantation and the relationships between bodies, places and inequality.

Research interests

Areas of inquiry

Gender & Health

Gendered experiences, care, bodily autonomy and the social determinants of health.

Living Organ Donation

Donor experiences, relational obligations, autonomy and ethical tensions in living donation.

Transnational Transplantation

Migration, medical return and the social and ethical dimensions of healthcare across borders.

Body & Ethics

Animal ethics, xenotransplantation and relationships between bodies, places and inequalities.
