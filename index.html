About

**Research Profile** I am an interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of gender, health, and ethics. My research examines how bodies, care, risk, and responsibility are negotiated in organ donation and transplantation. Drawing on qualitative and interdisciplinary approaches, I investigate the social relations, institutional arrangements, and ethical reasoning that shape bodily practices and medical decision-making. My current research focuses on transnational organ transplantation between Germany and Turkey, while my broader interests include living organ donation, xenotransplantation, and the social and ethical dimensions of bodily intervention.

Research projects

Current and forthcoming work

TIED · Germany and Turkey · 2027–2030

Transnational Organ Transplantation and Living Organ Donation: An Intersectional and Ethical Inquiry between Germany and Turkey

This project examines how Turkish-origin families navigate organ transplantation and living donation across Germany and Turkey. It explores how decisions are shaped by relationships, ethical reasoning, migration and healthcare contexts.

University of Bremen · Central Research Development Fund · Principal Investigator

BODYLINES · 2027–2030

Gender, Care and Transnational Experiences in Organ Transplantation

Research on gender, care and transnational experiences in organ transplantation.

Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR) · Postdoctoral Research Lead
